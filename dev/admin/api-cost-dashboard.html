What these numbers mean These are in-process LinkMia counters. They start after CORS, parsing, and rate-limit checks, so they are not a count of every connection Railway received. They can reset when the proxy restarts or when its 24-hour reset timer runs. A provider attempt is not proof of a billable SKU event, and this dashboard does not calculate or estimate charges.
